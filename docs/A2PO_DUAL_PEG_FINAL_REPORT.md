# A2PO Dual-Panda Dual-Peg Assembly Report

Date: 2026-08-24

## Executive status

The verified physical environment was preserved.  The earlier zero-force result was caused by collision-free policy trajectories and incomplete episode peak-force accounting, not by absent collision geometry.  Direct MuJoCo tests now show real peg/receiver contacts through `mujoco.mj_contactForce`, and the three manual impedance settings produce different physical responses.

This is **not declared research-complete**: the contact instrumentation and baseline comparison pass, but the contact-rich policy trained from the saved 200-episode checkpoint reaches only 6.4% success on the difficult formal evaluation.  The 98% collision-free result must not be used as evidence of compliant assembly.

The previous `render_a2po_final_videos.py` replay was identified as invalid for
the requested task: it directly reset the workpiece base pose along a scripted
trajectory and showed a horizontal receiver.  It is retained as an old
artifact, but is not a valid cooperative-grasp result.

The new scene is implemented in
`src/x_bimanual/panda_dynamic_vertical_assembly.py`.  It uses the same local
Franka/Panda meshes, a vertical X-Z receiver whose hole axes point along -Y, a
free receiver body with applied six-dimensional disturbance wrench, a free
workpiece at reset, and two grasp welds that are disabled until both arms reach
the workpiece sides.  The corrected replay is generated by
`scripts/render_dynamic_vertical_cooperative.py`; after the grasp event the
object is driven by two Panda-wrist fixed constraints while the receiver
undergoes bounded translation and rotation sway.

## Physical contact diagnosis

- `workpiece_peg1` and `workpiece_peg2` are cylinder geoms with `contype=1`, `conaffinity=1`, zero margin and zero gap.
- The receiver has 32 segmented convex `hole_ring_*` wall geoms plus the receiver frame, also with `contype=1`, `conaffinity=1`, zero margin and zero gap.
- Default contact friction is `0.7 0.05 0.01`; `solref="0.006 1"`, `solimp="0.88 0.96 0.01"`; timestep is `0.002 s`, implicit-fast integrator, elliptic cone.
- Two active equality welds (`left_grasp`, `right_grasp`) attach both Panda wrists to the same rigid workpiece.  No peg is attached to a hole.
- The aligned open passage has `ncon=0` and zero wrench.  Deliberate left-wall, right-wall, angular, and one-peg-aligned/one-peg-misaligned poses all have nonzero contacts.  Representative peak contact norms were 729 N, 729 N, 10.2 kN, and 753 N respectively.
- Force extraction calls `mujoco.mj_contactForce(model, data, contact_id, buffer)` for each peg contact and reports the norm of the summed contact-frame translational force.  The translational norm is frame-invariant; primitive cylinder/box contacts return zero contact torque, so the reported torque is correctly zero for these contacts.

Raw results: `results/contact_diagnostics.json`.

## Manual impedance response

All trials used the same runtime initial pose `[0.006, 0, 0.20]` with both pegs contacting the receiver wall and the same aligned object target.  The initial solver force was 3595 N in all trials.  Dynamic response differed:

| setting | dynamic peak force | final mean lateral error | lateral error std | final mean depth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| high stiffness | 1911 N | 13.50 mm | 3.50 mm | 0.034 mm |
| medium stiffness | 2143 N | 15.19 mm | 4.02 mm | 0.000 mm |
| low lateral stiffness / high damping | 1910 N | 17.45 mm | 4.15 mm | 0.000 mm |

This demonstrates that K/D values affect force and motion, while also showing that this particular manually forced overlap is difficult to recover.  Raw results: `results/contact_impedance_tests.json` and `.csv`.

## Training and formal evaluation

The curriculum keeps the verified robot/receiver/solver unchanged and widens only reset pose ranges: easy uses the original range, medium uses a receiver-mouth range, and difficult uses randomized `±6 mm`, `0.225–0.245 m` height, and `±0.06 rad` orientation.  Agent 2 receives the six-dimensional Agent 1 action through `A2POCoordinator`.

The sanity run used 100 episodes.  Actions stayed within bounds, all K/D values stayed positive, and the corrected peak-force statistic (including reset-time `mj_contactForce`) reached 22.3 kN on contact-rich samples.  Training reached episode 200 and saved `checkpoint_000200.pt`; the long difficult-stage run was stopped because many episodes consumed all 1600 steps without improving.  This is recorded rather than hidden.

Formal evaluation contains 500 independent episodes total, 125 per ablation, with a 600-step evaluation cap:

| variant | success | mean force | peak force | jamming | mean peg1 depth | mean peg2 depth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A RL trajectory + fixed impedance | 8.0% | 13.61 N | 4.04 kN | 52% | 29.71 mm | 30.16 mm |
| B learned variable impedance, unconditioned | 6.4% | 14.03 N | 4.12 kN | 48% | 23.52 mm | 23.98 mm |
| C prior-conditioned ablation | 6.4% | 14.03 N | 4.12 kN | 48% | 23.53 mm | 23.98 mm |
| D full A2PO | 6.4% | 14.03 N | 4.12 kN | 48% | 23.53 mm | 23.99 mm |

Position and orientation errors, insertion time, torque, both lateral peg errors, and both depths are in `results/a2po_dual_panda_final/formal_evaluation_summary.json` and `ablation_episodes.csv`.  The learned-variable policy does not yet demonstrate a higher success rate or lower jamming than fixed impedance; further contact-rich training is required before making that claim.

## Dynamic vertical continuation (2026-08-25)

The runtime weld capture bug was traced to the MuJoCo weld `eq_data` layout:
the runtime relative pose occupies indices `3:10`, while the previous code
wrote `0:7`.  Correcting this removed the large constraint residual at grasp.
The environment now uses collision-free pre-grasp initialization, a bounded
six-dimensional compliant suspension for the free receiver, and two
grasp-frame-consistent wrist impedance targets derived from one object pose.

The strict scripted MuJoCo demo now succeeds after a physical two-arm grasp:
peg lateral errors are 0.888 mm and 0.986 mm, insertion depths are 36.74 mm
and 36.59 mm, the 25-step hold condition passes, and peak contact force is
59.97 N.  No object pose is directly reset after grasp.

A separate dynamic checkpoint now exists at
`results/a2po_dual_panda_dynamic_vertical/checkpoint_final.pt`.  A 20-episode
three-stage curriculum reached 60% training success; the last ten training
episodes reached 70%, and a separate ten-episode difficult evaluation reached
40% success with zero recorded jamming.  This is a valid environment-specific
checkpoint, but the sample count is too small and 40% is too low to claim
research-level convergence.  The old `checkpoint_000200.pt` remains specific
to the horizontal `DualPandaAssemblyEnv`.
